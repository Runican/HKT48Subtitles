## Show
- 2026-10-29 (Thu), Team KIV 「ここにだって天使はいる」. One show: doors (lobby) 16:30, start 18:30 JST. Archive on LOD.
- Venue: UNITEDLAB (ユナイテッドラボ), 1-3-36 Daimyo, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka. Same stand-in venue as 10/28 (glossary `unitedlab`). Not the new theater.
