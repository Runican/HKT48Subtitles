## Rulings and traps
- **Blueberry Pie members:** Ryuto, Eura, Shibui + Takao, Ozaki (official X, Pia, Shibui's and Takemoto's posts). 48pedia and one blog list Kurihara Sae instead of Shibui; that is the 2014 original unit's name (Kurihara was in it) and is wrong for this show.
- **Romantic Byou:** Toyonaga/Jitoe + Kudo/Harada/Yoshida (Pia prints 冨永 for 豊永; typo). Costumes: Harada wore Tanaka Miku's, Kudo took Matsuoka Hana's position (their posts). 
- **Titles:** official X prints ウインクは3回 (Pia ウィンク) and ブルーベーリーパイ (typo for ブルーベリー); STU song 夢力 is read ゆめぢから; 3-2 is さんひくに.
- **Two Sayakas, two Yuukas, two Yuunas, Ria/Airi:** Takao Sayaka さーやん vs Harada Sayaka さやかりん; Kurushima Yuka くるくる/ゆうか vs HKT Eura Yuka ゆうか; Kawamata Yuuna ゆなゆな vs Ishihara Yuuna ゆーなん; Arai Ria りあちゅう vs Ichimura Airi あいちー. Nicks (fan wiki): Okada あずみん, Ozaki ぱせり/ぱちゃん (ASR パセリ), Kudo りこち, Ikeda 池ちゃん, Fukuda ふくちゃん/あかりん, Sogawa さっちー, Yoshida さらぴ, Morokuzu のあぴ, Hamada ひーちゃん, Kitazawa いちご, Watanabe なっちゃん.
- **HKT nick traps in this show:** Kurihara Sae ≠ Kuriyama Rina (Kurina); Eguchi Kokoha ここちゃん (the Fujino Kokoha is not on the cast); Izawa Miyu ざわちゃん; Imamura Maria まーさん; いぶくる = Ishibashi + Takemoto.
- **Hometown talk:** Takao and Yoshida (both Fukuoka-born STU members) say they were HKT fans: Yoshida went to almost every HKT event and handshake; Takao went to the HKT theater about monthly and was a fan of Murashige Anna (official report). Ozaki posted the Nagasaki-born members: Eguchi, Hamada, Ryuto, Ozaki. Harada is also Fukuoka-born (own post). Hiroshima touches: 揚げもみじ in dressing-room catering (Imamura, Ichimura posts).
- **Jargon:** 対バン, カメコ席/撮影OKタイム (photo-OK time), 練り歩き (aisle walk; H07/H08), STU48号 ship theater, 瀬戸内 (Setouchi), キャプテン = Okada Azumi (3rd captain since 2024-04-01), 副キャプテン = Fukuda Akari. Ishida Chiho (石田千穂) is not in Part 1; a mention is about her Part 2 role.
- **MC quotes (official report):** HKT congratulates STU on the 8th year ("Hakata and Setouchi are close yet rarely meet"); Ozaki says the STU pair were the oldest pair with over a dozen years' age gap to Eura (13), Shibui (16), Ryuto (14); Fukuda and Toyonaga's closing exchange (joint MC); Okada's final thanks. Order of the Yoshida/Takao talk inside the STU block is not sourced.

## Follow-up: JA doubts from the stitched draft (verifier, 2026-10-28)
- **ホーテット (about 0:27:03) is not a unit.** A second decode of the same audio gives "STU48さんの高尾さやかさん" (plain: "STU…さんの"). ホーテット is ASR for フォーティーエイト, so the line is "STU48's Takao Sayaka-san". The ASR kanji 高尾紗友香 is wrong; the member is 高雄さやか (stu48.com roster). Eguchi's own later post confirms the pair are friends (https://asukyann.blog.jp/archives/90301827.html).
- **Pちゃん is 駒田京伽 (Komada Hiroka), not 小間田ひろか.** She is a former HKT48 Team H member with the official nick ぴーちゃん (https://48pedia.org/駒田京伽) and one of the five original Blueberry Pie members (https://akb48taimuzu.livedoor.biz/archives/40610189.html). Ozaki Serika posted that she danced "推しメン・駒田京伽さんのポジション" (https://asukyann.blog.jp/archives/89573193.html; her 2021 birthday talk: https://setouchidiary.hatenablog.com/entry/2021/11/18/190000). So the speaker at about 1:03:26 is Ozaki, and the next voice (ASR サーヤ, about 1:03:38) is Takao Sayaka, さーやん (Takemoto's post prints さーやんさん with Takao's handle: https://asukyann.blog.jp/archives/89563081.html).
- **田島メル is 田島芽瑠, read Tashima Meru** (48pedia, Wikipedia). She is a former HKT48 member (graduated 2022), not an STU48 member. Yoshida Sara has named her as her oshi (2022 radio talk: http://blog.livedoor.jp/radio4846talk/archives/40608058.html). Harada wore Tanaka Miku's costume and Kudo took Matsuoka Hana's position, so the speaker at about 1:02:46 is most likely Yoshida in Tashima's place (PROV; Yoshida's own post not found).
- **くるたん = Takemoto Kurumi** (official nick, https://www.hkt48.jp/profile/87). The "Kurutan, Ibuki" line at about 0:35:13 is the Ibukuru pair.
- **Fukuda Akari is the STU member at about 1:14:05** whose name Toyonaga said in MC2 (about 0:33:14: STU48's Fukuda Akari-chan). The official report has Toyonaga's line "福岡も瀬戸内海と面している" after Fukuda's call for more exchange (https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000072.000136906.html). The 兼任 line is Fukuda's joke; Toyonaga is HKT48 only. Geography is sound: Fukuoka Prefecture touches the Seto Inland Sea at Suō-nada (https://www.env.go.jp/content/900501132.pdf).
- **Izawa's haircut (about 0:34:14)**: Izawa herself says she cut 20 cm "for today". No outside source; render as spoken.
- **Not resolved (ear):** ダンジョン (about 0:27:33; a second decode hears 壇上, the gist is that she ranked high among Takao's SHOWROOM fans; no SHOWROOM "dungeon" feature found), ギブスで (about 0:30:02, 1 s), 割り当て (about 0:30:23, both decodes agree but the sense is odd after the fishnets question), "48章" (about 0:33:37, no source for the 2019 event), and "Saya-chan" (about 0:28:10; the only HKT name close to this is Kurihara Sae, さえちゃん).
- **Closing:** 4930-4936 "ご視聴ありがとうございました" is a Whisper silence hallucination (it appears in plain decode at the end with no speech). The floor call is 3階席, 2階席, バルコニー (not "2階席" twice).
- **Source discrepancy:** the PR TIMES setlist prints the Blueberry Pie HKT trio as 龍頭・江浦・栗原; the official X, Pia and the Liveen Times copy of the same text say 渋井美奈. Keep Shibui.
