## Common deviations (low-confidence flags)

- Unit understudies: 48pedia lists many alternate casts per unit slot; do not assume shonichi unit cast.
- Opening sketch title/content changes monthly; do not hardcode one sketch title as the stage template.
- Birthday / graduation / shin-pozi shows may insert talk or rare song swaps — treat as show-specific.
- Cast is 16 per show; kenkyuusei often understudy; absences announced on schedule.
- LOD vs theater: same song order; camera/MC length may differ; LOD may trim some talk.
- Official desktop setlist page once omitted the **エンドロール** heading visually (composer 鮫島巧 still present); Mobile + 48pedia + fandom confirm the song is M05 — do not drop it.
- Romaji variants: Stage48 `Sakaagari` / CD `SAKAAGARI` — **prefer locked glossary `Saka Agari`** (space; reject `Sakagari`).
