## Show
- 2026-10-28 (Wed), Team KIV 「ここにだって天使はいる」 (Koko ni Datte Tenshi wa Iru). Only one show that day: doors (lobby) 16:30, start 18:30 JST. Archive-only on LOD (no live stream).
- Venue: UNITEDLAB (ユナイテッドラボ), a live house at 1-3-36 Daimyo, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka. It is a **stand-in venue, not a new theater**. The official title is 「HKT48劇場公演 UNITEDLAB開催」.
- Background: the 17LIVE HKT48 Theater (17LIVE HKT48劇場, BOSS E・ZO FUKUOKA 1F, open since 2020-11-02) closed after its 2026-07-26 shows. A new theater in 010 BUILDING (Sumiyoshi, Hakata-ku) was due to open in autumn 2026, but was postponed on 2026-09-30 with no new date. The UNITEDLAB run lasts 6 days: 10/28 and 10/29 KIV Tenshi, 10/30 H Mokugekisha, 10/31 KIV Tenshi 12:30 and H Mokugekisha 17:00, 11/2 Ishibashi Ibuki solo event, 11/3 H Mokugekisha (Ishibashi graduation).
- So this is the first stage show since the old theater closed (a gap of about 3 months). It is not a new home theater.
