## Follow-up ruling: Kurina's Oita catch
Ear review of this show hears かぼす (kabosu), not ボス. This agrees with her 2019 self-intro text (温泉・しいたけ・かぼす) and with kabosu being an Oita specialty. The glossary entry `kuriyama-rina` now uses 温泉、しいたけ、なし、かぼす for all her intros, replacing the earlier Boss-coffee reading for the 250506 Tenshi show; earlier per-show notes that say Boss or "do not lock kabosu" are superseded.
