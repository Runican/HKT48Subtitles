## Title
Official: LIVEイベント「～音の波 言の葉～」. 波 is read ハ: the member hashtag is #オトノハコトノハ and fan text gives おとのはことのは. The official hashtag is #音の波言の葉. No official English title. Romaji: Oto no Ha, Koto no Ha (Otonoha Kotonoha is fine as a slug). 「音のは 言のは」 is wrong. It is a one-off event planned by Sakamoto Erena, not a regular 公演, with two shows on 2025-04-13 (12:30 and 17:00).
